The question

Where will the next eight weeks of work get stuck, and what is the smallest change that fixes it?

What the scenarios showed

One extra evening operator on Welding Cell 2 clears every overloaded week. Moving 10% of welds to Welding Cell 1 only moves the problem: Cell 1 goes over capacity in 7 of 8 weeks.
