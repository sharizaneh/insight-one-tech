About

Manufacturing intelligence built for the way your plant actually runs.

Insight One Tech helps steel fabricators and manufacturing job shops turn the data they already collect into clear decisions on capacity, constraints and delivery, grounded in real plant-floor experience.

Request a Manufacturing Data AssessmentSee services

Why it matters

Most plants don't have a data problem. They have a decision problem.

Plants already record what they need: machine hours, orders, labour, ship dates. It just sits in separate systems and arrives too late to change the week. Insight One Tech exists to close that gap with reporting, capacity models and automation that a production team will actually use.

The approach comes from hands-on experience in structural steel fabrication: production data spread across separate systems, capacity questions answered from memory, and teams that need a straight answer more than another dashboard. Insight One is developed independently, and every demonstration on this site uses synthetic data.

For you, that means dashboards that follow your orders, work centers, shifts and ship dates; recommendations you can act on this week; and results your own team can act on, explained in plain language, with your data always exportable.
