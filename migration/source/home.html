Most plants don’t have a data problem. They have a decision problem. We connect the numbers you already collect to the choices you make every week: what to run, what to quote, and where to add capacity.

Where plants lose time

The data exists. It just doesn’t answer your questions yet.

Most manufacturers already track hours, orders, and output. The problem is that the numbers live in separate files and arrive too late to change the week.

  • Every department keeps its own spreadsheet.

    Which version of the backlog is the right one?

  • Weekly reports take hours of copying and pasting.

    What did we actually ship last week, by product line and by work center?

  • Capacity forecasts are educated guesses.

    Can we take this order and still ship everything else on time?

  • Bottlenecks show up as late shipments.

    Where is work waiting right now, and for how long?

  • No shared view of the plant floor.

    Are we on plan today, without walking the floor to find out?

What Insight One shows you

Four answers from the data you already have.

Your exports are mapped to one standard layout, checked for quality, then analysed. Results come as dashboards and exports, and can feed Power BI or the reporting tool you already use.

CAPACITY

Capacity & utilization

Available, used and remaining hours by work center, shift and week, instead of estimates from experience.

  • Utilization and downtime by work center
  • Remaining capacity before each due date
  • Throughput and jobs completed

CONSTRAINTS

Bottleneck & downtime intelligence

The work center that limits throughput, and what is eating its time, before it becomes overtime or a missed ship date.

  • Primary constraint with a plain-language reason
  • Downtime by cause and trend
  • Work center and shift comparison

FORECAST

Production forecasting

Expected load against available hours for the coming weeks, with a likely range instead of a single guess.

  • Weekly load versus capacity
  • Weeks at risk flagged early
  • Ship-date risk for open orders

WHAT-IF

Scenario testing

Test a change before you make it, and see where the constraint moves next.

  • An extra operator, shift or overtime
  • Reduced downtime at the constraint
  • Moving suitable work between cells

      Questions

      Before you get in touch

      What is a Manufacturing Data Assessment?

      A short, no-commitment first step. You share one or two production exports (ERP, MES, timesheet or spreadsheet) through a private upload link we send you, not by email. We map them to a standard layout, score the data quality, and walk you through a first view of utilization, downtime and the likely constraint in a 30-minute session.

      Does our data need to be clean before we start?

      No. Most manufacturers start with spreadsheets, ERP exports, and timesheet reports that don’t line up perfectly. Part of the work is cleaning and connecting them, and showing you which gaps matter.

      What systems do you work with?

      Anything that can export data: ERP and production-management systems, time and attendance software, Excel files, and databases. Results come as clear dashboards and exports your team can use, and can feed Power BI or the reporting tool you already have.

      Do you work on-site?

      Yes, where it helps, for example to walk the floor and understand how work moves between stations. Most of the build and follow-up happens remotely, so we can work with plants anywhere.

      Who owns the data and the results?

      Your production data stays yours. You can export it and your results at any time, so you are never locked in. The Insight One platform, its models and its methods remain Insight One’s; for project work, deliverables and licence terms are set out in each agreement.

      Is AI or machine learning required?

      Usually not at the start. Good reporting and capacity planning solve most problems. Machine learning is recommended only when there is enough history and a clear benefit over simpler methods.
